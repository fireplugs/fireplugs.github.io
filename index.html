Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about SmartTab, your custom new tab for Chrome.

What is SmartTab?

SmartTab is a free Chrome extension that replaces your default new tab page with a customizable productivity dashboard. Add widgets like to-do lists, weather, Pomodoro timer, stocks, crypto, Gmail, and more.

Is SmartTab really free?

Yes — 100% free with no premium tier, no ads on your dashboard, and no credit card required. Every widget and feature is included. Install from Chrome Web Store.

Can I customize the layout?

Absolutely. Drag and resize widgets to build your ideal layout. Choose from 4 color themes, upload custom backgrounds, and toggle any widget on or off. Settings sync across your Chrome devices.

Does SmartTab collect my data?

Your data stays in your browser. Gmail access is optional and uses Google OAuth. We don't sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

What widgets are included?

To-do list, Pomodoro focus timer, weather, stocks, cryptocurrency, Gmail, notes, passwords, quick links, world clocks, daily quotes, calendar, clock, top sites, and a built-in search bar.

How do I install SmartTab?

Click "Add to Chrome" on the Chrome Web Store page. Open a new tab and your dashboard is ready — customize it in under a minute.
